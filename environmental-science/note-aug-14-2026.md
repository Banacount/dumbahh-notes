### Hydrosphere (water)

Heat - hot air from the sun.
Condensation - body of water to clouds because of heat.
Transpiration - water from the leaves to clouds because of heat.
Sublimation - water from snows in glazers.
Deposition - the geological process where moving water (such as rivers, streams, waves, and ocean currents).

### Lithosphere (land)

Layers:
* Crust
* Mantle
* Core (inner & outer)
* Asthenosphere
