## Environment

__Environ (French term)__ -> "surrounding" in english 

__Biotic (Living things)__ - Animals, Organisms and Humans.
__Abiotic (Non-living things)__ - Air, Land and Water.

#### Environment Definition:
- A complex system that includes all living (biotic) and
non-living (abiotic) elements surrounding organisms.

> These systems are interconnected and constantly changes, especially
due to human activities.
---
### Hydrosphere (watch videos on YT for further knowledge)
Includes all forms of water on earth.
> Examples: Lakes, ponds, rivers, streams, oceans

The water circulates through the environment with the hydrological cycle,
connecting atmosphere, land, and water bodies.

__Importance of the Hydrosphere__: 
- Supports aquatic life
- Essential for agriculture
- Regulates climate
- Provides water for human needs (drinking, sanitation, industry)

__Importance of the Hydrosphere__: 
- Water pollution
- Overuse of freshwater
- Ocean acidification
