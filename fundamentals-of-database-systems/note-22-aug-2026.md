### Elements  of the Database Approach

> Data models
* Graphical system capturing nature and relatioship of data.
* __Enterprice Data Model__ - high-level entities and relatioships for the organization.
* __Project Data Model__ - more detailed view, matching data structure in database or data warehouse.

> Entities
* Noun form describing a person, place, object, event, or concept.
* Composed attributes.

> Relationships
* Between entities.
* Usually one-to-many (1:M) or many-to-many (M:N).

> Relational databases
* Database technology involving tables (relations)

> Primary Key(PK) - Is unique and cannnot be duplicated.
> Foreign Key(FK) - Is unique but can be duplicated.

__EDM__ - Enterprise Data Model (high level, less detailed, big picture)
__PDM__ - Project-level Data Model (details of the EDM, detailed asf)

#### Enterprise Model
* High-level
* Less detail
* Big picture
* Major entities
* Major relationships

#### Project Model
* More detailed
* Shows attributes
* Closer to actual database design
* Includes more specific structures
