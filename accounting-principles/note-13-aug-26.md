### Introduction to accounting
* Bookkeeping - the daily process of recording, organizing, tracking expenses.
* Acounting - the system of recording, sorting, and summarizing money that comes in and goes out of a business.

#### Basic Uses of Accounting
1. Comparing prices
2. Managing Debt
3. Household accounting
4. Running a small business

#### Accounting encourages deeper questions:
* How much will remain after the purchase?
* Will there be enough for emergency savings?
* Is this really needed?

#### Why is accounting important?:
1. Planning
2. Controlling
3. Decision-Making
4. Accountability
5. Evaluation

#### What makes it useful?:
1. Timely
2. Understandable
3. Comparable

#### Who needs accounting information?:
1. Family
2. Managers
3. Investors
4. Government
4. Creditors/Lenders
