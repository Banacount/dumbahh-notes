
**Economic Benefit** - The potential for an asset to contribute either directly or
indirectly to the flow of an entity's cash.

**Credit** (The source of Economic Benefit)
**Debit** (The destination of Economic Benefit)

> Assets
```txt
Debit      Credit
  |           |
  V           V
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
```

> Equity
```txt
Equity = Owner's equity - Dividends + Retained Earnings
        |
        V
Retained Earnings = Revenue - Expenses
        |
        V
  Equity = Owner's equity - Dividends + Revenue - Expenses
        |
        V
  Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity - Dividends + Revenue - Expenses 
        |
        V
  Dividends + Expenses + Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity + Revenue
   |                         |     |                                  |
   ---------------------------     ------------------------------------
                 |                                       |
                 V                                       V
            Debits (LHS)                            Credits (RHS)
```
> D E A L E R

**Left Hand Side** (LHS) - Increase when debited and Decrease when credited.
**Right Hand Side** (RHS) - Decrease when debited and Increase when credited.

**Debits and Credits reflect the duality of all financial transactions.**
